# Intermediate Representations in Imperative Compilers: A Survey

- James Stanier and Des Watson
- 2013
- [ACM DL](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2480741.2480743)
- [alternate link](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?repid=rep1&type=pdf&doi=74db5beee87ef7abdb77eb4dd23e13fdaf12c77a)

This paper provides a survey of intermediate representations (IRs) used in imperative compilers.
It's a longer paper, but with little formalism, so it should be a quicker read.
Don't get stuck on the details of any one IR; 
 the goal is to understand the commonalities and differences between them.
